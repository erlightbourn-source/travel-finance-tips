Trip Receipts · Fort Worth

A cattle drive, a cheap flight, and a hotel room for zero

Hyatt Place Fort Worth Stockyards2025Free-night awardAA from Florida

Not every points trip is a beach or a skyline. This one was Texas — the Fort Worth Historic Stockyards, the twice-daily cattle drive, and a room at the Hyatt Place that we booked on a World of Hyatt free-night award and paid nothing for.

The everyday redemption

A Hyatt Place isn't a palace, and that's the point. Free nights aren't only for aspirational hotels. Dropping one on a solid, well-located room in a market you're visiting anyway — near family, near the thing you came to see — is a perfectly good use of the benefit. The confirmation reads STANDARD ROOM FREE NIGHT, King bed, nothing owed.

Pair it with a cheap flight and the whole trip gets light: American runs straight to Dallas–Fort Worth from South Florida, and the room was already handled by a night you'd earned.

Free nights aren't just for splurges

The World of Hyatt card earns a free night every year that works as well on a Hyatt Place near family as it does on a resort. Chase points transfer in one-to-one to keep the balance topped up.
