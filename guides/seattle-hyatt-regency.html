Trip Receipts · Seattle

A free night in the biggest hotel in the Pacific Northwest

Hyatt Regency SeattleApril 2024Free-night awardExplorist
What the room cost
0 dollars

One night at the Hyatt Regency Seattle, booked on a World of Hyatt free-night award. The confirmation lists it as an award stay — no cash rate to pay, checked in as an Explorist member.

The Hyatt Regency Seattle is a 1,260-room tower a block from the convention center — the kind of big, modern downtown hotel that runs a healthy nightly rate whenever the city has anything going on. We stayed on a free night and walked out having paid for the room exactly nothing.

Why this is the redemption that makes sense

This is the flip side of the Santa Monica trade. A downtown Regency in a convention city is precisely where cash rates spike and an award night earns its keep. Same points that would have been wasted on a cheap roadside room turned into a night in the middle of Seattle instead.

What Explorist added

Checking in with mid-tier World of Hyatt status isn't flashy, but it changes the edges of a stay in ways you feel:

  • Late checkout, so the last morning isn't a scramble
  • Bonus points on top of the base earning, even on an award stay's incidentals
  • The upgrade lottery actually tilts your way at check-in

The card behind it

The free night and the status both trace back to the same place — the World of Hyatt credit card, which hands you a category-tier free night every year on your anniversary and pushes you toward Explorist just for using it. One night like this most years pays the fee on its own.

Turn a card anniversary into a night like this

The free night that covered downtown Seattle is the same benefit that covers a resort or a big-city tower. It's worth the most exactly where cash hurts the most.
