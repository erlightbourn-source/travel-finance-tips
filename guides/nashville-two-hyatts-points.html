A Nashville weekend across two Hyatts, and we paid for neither room

Grand Hyatt NashvilleHyatt House DowntownOctober 2023Free-night awards

Nashville on a weekend is not cheap. Rooms downtown spike whenever there's a game, a bachelorette party, or, honestly, most Fridays. We spent a couple of nights there across two different Hyatts and paid the room rate at neither one.

Both nights went on World of Hyatt free-night awards, split across two properties a short walk apart:

Grand Hyatt Nashville The splashy one — rooftop, big lobby, right downtown
Free-night award
Hyatt House Downtown Suite-style, kitchen, a softer landing the second night
Free-night award

Why split it

There's no rule that both free nights have to burn at the same hotel. Splitting the stay let one night be the flashy downtown tower and the next be a roomier suite with a kitchen, and neither one cost a dollar. When the currency is award nights, variety is free.

The confirmation on the Hyatt House night listed a cash rate near two hundred dollars for the room we didn't pay for. That's the tell: the award quietly absorbed a real, printed number.
