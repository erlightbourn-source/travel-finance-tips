The Santa Monica stay we paid cash for — and why that was the right move

Hyatt Centric DelfinaOctober 2025Member rateWorld of Hyatt

Most of what we write about here is award nights — rooms booked for points instead of cash. This one wasn't, on purpose. Sometimes the free night isn't the smart night, and Santa Monica in early October was a clean example of that.

We spent three nights at the Hyatt Centric Delfina, two blocks off the beach and a short walk from the pier. Here's what it actually cost, and the reasoning behind not touching a single point for it.

Room: two queens, partial ocean view with a balcony. Plus a $35/night destination fee and city tax. Booked on the World of Hyatt member rate — the small discount you get just for being signed in.

Why not use points here

The Centric is a mid-tier Hyatt. A free night here would have cost roughly the same number of points as a night at a hotel charging two or three times as much in cash. Spending an award on a $270 room is how points quietly go to waste. So we paid the member rate, kept the points, and earned more of them on the stay instead.

The rule we actually follow: use points where the cash price is ugly, pay cash where it's reasonable. A free night is only a good deal measured against what the room would have cost — not against zero.

Getting there

We flew Delta for this one, into LAX and back out to Miami, and the trip put a couple thousand SkyMiles back in the account on the return alone. Nothing exotic on the flights — the point was to keep the trip cheap where it made sense and not overthink the parts that were already fair.

The card angle

Even on a paid stay, the World of Hyatt card earns on every dollar and keeps your status where late checkout and the odd upgrade come standard. The member rate itself is free to anyone with a World of Hyatt account. You don't need elite anything to get it — you need to be signed in when you book.

Where paying beats redeeming

Points are a tool, not a scoreboard. The card that earns and holds status on the nights you do pay for is the same one that unlocks the free nights when they're worth it.
