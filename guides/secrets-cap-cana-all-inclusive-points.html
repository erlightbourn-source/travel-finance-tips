The best-kept secret in Hyatt points is the all-inclusive

Secrets Cap CanaJuly 2024Adults-only · all-inclusiveFree-night award

When people picture booking a hotel with points, they picture a room. A bed, a shower, maybe a view. What they don't picture is a resort where the food, the drinks, the swim-up bar, and the beach are all already paid for the moment you walk in. That's what an all-inclusive on points actually is, and it's the quietest great deal in the whole hobby.

Secrets Cap Cana is an adults-only resort on the eastern tip of the Dominican Republic, part of Hyatt's all-inclusive collection. We booked it on a World of Hyatt free-night award — the confirmation shows it as an award stay, no room charge — and everything the resort does was folded in from there.

The food & drinks
All included

A points night at a normal hotel saves you the room rate. A points night at an all-inclusive saves you the room, the restaurant, and the bar tab — for the whole stay.

Why all-inclusives break the math in your favor

At a city hotel, the room is most of what you pay, so a free room is most of the savings. At a resort like this, the room is only the doorway. Once you're in, a normal day would have meant a steady drip of restaurant bills and bar rounds, and none of that lands. The award doesn't just cover a room — it switches off the meter on the entire trip.

How it connects to the rest

This is the same play we ran at Hyatt Zilara in Jamaica, and it's not a coincidence. Hyatt is unusual among the big hotel programs in letting you point straight at all-inclusive resorts with award nights. That single quirk is most of the reason the World of Hyatt card ends up worth carrying.

The card that reaches the all-inclusives

Most hotel points can't touch resorts like this. World of Hyatt can — and Chase points transfer into it one-to-one, which is how the free nights get funded in the first place.
