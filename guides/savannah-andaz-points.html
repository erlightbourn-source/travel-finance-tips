Trip Receipts · Savannah

A boutique Andaz on the Savannah river, on a free night

Andaz SavannahJuly 2024Free-night awardExplorist

Savannah is the kind of place people underrate on points, because it's boutique, not big-box. The Andaz sits right on the historic riverfront, a couple of blocks from the squares everyone comes to walk. We booked it on a World of Hyatt free-night award and stayed for no cash.

Boutique doesn't mean off-limits

Hyatt's smaller lifestyle brands — Andaz among them — are bookable on the same free nights as the giant convention hotels. In a walkable, in-demand old town like Savannah, that's a strong use of a night: a characterful hotel in the exact spot you want to be, at a cash rate you skip entirely.

This one was a shorter stop than most of our trips, but the math doesn't care how long you stay. One night in the right place, for free, is still one night in the right place, for free.

A quick look

From the trip — the riverfront and the shops along it. A short stay, so a short gallery, honestly.

Use a free night on the boutique stays too

The World of Hyatt free night reaches Andaz and Hyatt's other lifestyle hotels, not just the big towers. It's worth the most wherever the cash rate is highest — including a walkable historic riverfront.
