Card math

Book flights direct for 5x, not 1x

Same ticket, same card, five times the points. The only thing that changes is where you click Book.

The rule

The Platinum Card from American Express earns 5x Membership Rewards points on flights booked directly with the airline or through Amex Travel, on up to $500,000 of those purchases per calendar year. Book the same flight through a third-party travel site and it earns the card's base 1x.

The math on one ticket

A $600 round trip booked on the airline's website earns 3,000 points. Booked through a third-party site, it earns 600.

At a conservative 1 cent per point that's $30 against $6. If you transfer Membership Rewards to airline partners and get 1.5 to 2 cents per point, the gap grows to $45 to $60 on a single ticket. Across a year of flights it adds up fast.

Two more reasons to book direct

Changes and cancellations are simpler. When the booking sits with the airline, you deal with one company instead of two.

Your airline status and seat perks attach cleanly. Third-party bookings sometimes cause trouble with seat selection and upgrades.

Is the card worth it for you?

The Platinum carries an $895 annual fee. The 5x on flights helps, but it's only one piece. Run the full keep-or-cancel test before you decide.
